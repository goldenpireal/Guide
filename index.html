Why INT MΛP exists

The internet has the answers.
We’re bringing the paths together.

You can learn almost anything online. There are brilliant tutorials, courses, videos, books and communities all over the internet. But they’re scattered across different websites, and figuring out where to begin can be overwhelming.

That’s why we’re building INT MΛP. Search for a topic and find a complete roadmap to help you master it, with useful sources gathered in one place. Our goal is to make those resources free to access and open source wherever possible, so learning doesn’t depend on how much money you have.

Learning should be FUN and FREE.

A little about us

Less time hunting for resources. More time actually learning.

INT MΛP is a project built around a simple belief: curiosity should be enough to get started. You shouldn’t need to know ten different websites or pay for a dozen subscriptions just to begin learning something new.

We’re bringing learning paths and useful resources into one place, and we hope to grow it with a community of people who love sharing what they know.
